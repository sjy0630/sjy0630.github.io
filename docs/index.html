About

I'm an undergraduate at the College of Integrated Circuits and Micro-Nano Electronics, Fudan University (entered 2025). My interests are computer architecture, digital IC design, and AI hardware acceleration — in particular, how algorithms and hardware can be designed together so models run efficiently on chips.

In research, I've worked on physical modeling and simulator development for 2D-material memristors, and on Transformer-based causal inference. Outside class I'm studying CS61C (computer architecture) and MIT 6.5940 (efficient AI computing), and beginning to learn CUDA and GPU architecture.

This site collects my projects and notes, and I update it as I go.

03

Projects

  • VULCAN-2D ↗

    Research · open source

    A multi-scale modeling framework and simulator for 2D-material memristors (Fudan Xiyuan undergraduate research program). It covers conductive-filament evolution, charge transport, Joule heating, and device variability; I worked on numerical modeling, I–V simulation, and variability analysis.

    Takeaway: fitting the data doesn't mean a model explains the physics — it still has to be tested against experiments.

    • Memristors
    • Physical modeling
    • Simulation

  • Causal Transformer

    Research · in progress

    Research on Transformer-based causal inference and active experiment design: can a model learn latent causal rules from offline data and choose its own experiments? I handle data generation, training, and evaluation in PyTorch, and have analyzed distribution shift in closed-loop decisions.

    Takeaway: code that runs and metrics that improve are still a long way from a trustworthy result.

    • Causal inference
    • Active experiment design
    • PyTorch

  • AEC GPGPU ↗

    Agentic4Systems contest · Third prize

    A GPGPU design for a custom 128-bit fixed-length ISA (AEC), built in four days for Track B of the 2026 Agentic4Systems Summer School contest; our team of three won third prize. I worked across the full flow: the C reference model, RTL implementation, RTL/C-model alignment, and synthesis and PPA tuning on the ASAP7 flow.

    Takeaway: ISA semantics, reference model, RTL, and PPA are tightly coupled — a change in one has to be carried through the rest.

    • GPGPU
    • C model
    • RTL
    • Synthesis
    • PPA

  • Tank Trouble

    Hardware · Course project

    An FPGA tank game built in Verilog on a Basys3 board for Digital Logic Design (Honors). I designed a modular RTL system integrating PS/2 keyboard input, 640×480 VGA rendering, ROM-based sprites with 32 tank orientations, fixed-point movement and rotation, and configurable maze rendering.

    • Verilog
    • FPGA
    • Vivado
    • VGA
    • PS/2

  • Raspberry Pi Smart Car ↗

    Embodied AI · Course project

    A vision- and speech-controlled robotic vehicle for Introduction to Embodied Intelligence. I built a PC-side pipeline integrating YOLOv5 detection, OpenCV tracking, PID steering, and speech commands, communicating with the Raspberry Pi over HTTP. I explored reduced detection frequency and frame-to-frame tracking to improve real-time performance.

    Takeaway: perception, latency, and feedback control have to work together for AI to act reliably in the physical world.

    • Python
    • YOLOv5
    • OpenCV
    • Raspberry Pi
    • PID
